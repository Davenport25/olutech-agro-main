Who We Are

Structured supply support for buyers who need clarity before commitment.

Olutech Agro Limited is a Nigerian agro-export company focused on sourcing, preparation, packaging, and buyer-aligned supply of agricultural commodities for international and institutional markets.

We support buyer conversations with practical details: grade expectations, moisture targets, packaging options, destination requirements, documentation planning, and realistic supply timelines.
